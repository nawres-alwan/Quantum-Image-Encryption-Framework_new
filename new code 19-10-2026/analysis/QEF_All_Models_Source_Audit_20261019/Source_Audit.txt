Scope

has been replaced and no extra encryption layer is introduced. The file
source_certification.tex supplies a rigorous criterion for the original
source and explains which information is unavailable. This audit is not a
certificate of a positive numerical resistance for the actual generator.

PSB sign error and proof
Original lines974-989 define arg(<psi_a|psi_b>)+integral Im(<psi|psidot>).
Under psi'(t)=exp(i chi(t))psi(t), the first term gains chi_b-chi_a and
the second gains the same quantity. The printed expression gains twice
that amount, so it is not gauge invariant as claimed at line1015.

The gauge-invariant expression is
gamma_g = arg(<psi_a|psi_b>) - integral Im(<psi|psidot>) dt
        = arg(<psi_a|psi_b>) + integral <H>/hbar dt.
Change plus to minus in eq:psb_phase_hgphi and minus to plus in
eq:psb_phase_hamiltonian_hgphi. Primary reference:
Rabei, Arvind, Mukunda and Simon, Bargmann Invariants and Geometric
Phases -- A Generalised Connection, Eq.(2.4), PDF page5:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/9907080
The original kinematic treatment is Mukunda and Simon, Annals of Physics
228 (1993),205-268, DOI10.1006/aphy.1993.1093.

Exact countercheck: let H=E I and psi(t)=exp(-iEt/hbar)psi0. The physical
ray is constant and its geometric phase is zero. The printed expression
instead gives -2E tau/hbar. With E tau/hbar=pi/4 and delta=0, its raw-bit
probability is1/2 while the corrected geometric phase gives probability1.

Impact tracing for that correction
The state evolution and the unitarity of the three encryption layers do
not change. The derived PSB phase, compact phi, Bernoulli probabilities,
raw-bit distribution and derived permutation/entanglement-key law can
change. Earlier numerical HG-Phi data cannot be silently attributed to
the corrected formula. The rotation-moment reference theorem allows any
joint B law and is unaffected as a conditional theorem; it still requires
the stated conditional moments of A given B. Correcting the PSB signs
does not establish these moments or substitution-angle uniformity.

Source-certification mathematics
The wrapped density is sum_m f_q(z+m pi). Gamma2 and uniform wrapped
density suffice for the one-pass continuous marginal on[0,2pi); modulo
does not establish it. Actual conditional moments delta2 and eta2 give
C_actual=ln[1+2^r delta2^2+(N-1)2^r eta2^2]. For independently refreshed
whole records, binary discrimination success p requires
q >= ceil[(ln2-h(p))/C_actual]. Distinct position blocks are orthogonal
in Hilbert--Schmidt inner product, giving the stated bound without
requiring independence within a record. Without certified source moments,
the general bound supplies onlyq>=1 atp=2/3; this is not a successful
attack and does not establish the actual attack cost.

Finite independent-symbol law versus weaker moments
Two independent uniform pixel tuples require6r log2(L) conditional
entropy bits. A deterministic s-bit source has pair support<=2^s and
distance to the uniform pair law at least max(0,1-2^s/L^(6r)). QIRBP r11,
s128 gives15/16 atL4 and1-2^(-400) atL256. This precludes asserting that
strong law from that seed, but does not preclude a correlated source
with adequate operator moments. No AFQIRHSI feature count is used here.

Raw bits and entropy
Given phase and setting, raw-bit pointwise guessing probability is
(1+abs(cos(phi+delta)))/2, which can equal1. Fixed deterministic
postprocessing cannot increase source Shannon or min-entropy; reversible
processing preserves it. A specified extractor needs an entropy bound,
seed assumptions, output length and extraction error. Statistical tests
and one-bit balance do not supply a uniform joint-law certificate.

Architecture countermodel
Constant FRQI feature|0> versus|1>, theta_x=theta_y=0, fresh theta_z,
any position permutation and all-CZ entanglement preserve the feature
Z label. One ciphertext gives perfect binary discrimination. This source
law is permitted by the unrestricted architecture, not attributed to
the actual proposed source. It proves that freshness and unitarity alone
cannot imply a nontrivial architecture-wide attack lower bound.
