The small-system channel calculation enumerates all substitution keys
on four models and compares the resulting superoperator with the
derived moment formula. It checks the full channel, rather than only
the norm of a few outputs. The image checks then verify trace norm,
Hilbert--Schmidt norm, and relative-entropy inequalities. Joint binary
measurements are evaluated directly on small product states and checked
against the Holevo/Fano converse. Separate perturbation cases check the
source-error inequality. These checks support the algebra but cannot
certify an unspecified HCS source distribution.

\section{Impact on the manuscript and remaining work}
The revision replaces the old quantum-attack subsection and its
``passed'' table. Its abstract, discussion, comparisons, and conclusion
are adjusted to the proved scope. Pixel-dependent angle indexing and
the chronological Euler/inverse order are made consistent with the
operator used by the theorem. The receiver uses the delivered stochastic
key values rather than drawing new QRNG outcomes. Hardware statements
distinguish small IBM circuits from the large-image simulations.

The three encryption layers and both proposed original key-source
classes remain in the mathematical architecture. No QOTP layer or v2
uniform privacy core is used in this derivation. A quantum-safe
confidentiality claim for the actual original source still requires a
certified finite joint angle/control law, the actual range parameter,
an imperfection bound, and implementation correspondence. The sorting
work-register cleanup must also be checked independently; the theorem
assumes an ideal clean address permutation. The new proof has not been
formalized in Lean~4.

A particularly important consequence is the range countermodel in
Section~\ref{sec:qef_moment_security}. With the independent uniform
$[0,\pi)$ reference, the expected sample counts for the demonstrated
postselection attack are only $65,536$, $50,394$, $65,536$, and $786,432$
for the four images, at success $0.702642$. Those are attack upper
bounds in that contrasting law; they are not the theorem's lower bounds
and are not measured attacks on the original generator. The sharp
difference prevents assigning one of the reference numbers to the
actual system before its angle law is established.
